Training data for GlycoTwin
Generated from the local Synthea sample. CGMacros was not used.

patients_baseline.csv
  One row per Synthea patient who has diabetes or prediabetes.
  Age, sex, BMI, blood pressure, and medicines come from the Synthea CSVs.
  hba1c_raw is the latest Synthea HbA1c, even when that value is not in a
  diabetic range. This sample's diabetes labs often sit near 2% to 5%.
  hba1c is the value used to simulate glucose.
  hba1c_source is synthea_lab when the lab was already plausible.
  It is imputed_from_diagnosis when the lab was missing or below 6.5%
  for a diabetes diagnosis (drawn uniformly from 6.6 to 8.4), or outside
  5.6 to 6.4 for prediabetes.

signals.csv
  Simulated 5-minute wearable rows for 14 days, seed 2026.
  Same patient_id as patients_baseline.csv.
  Fasting glucose is 28.7 x hba1c - 46.7 - 28 (ADAG average glucose,
  shifted down so the night sits below the average).
  Meals at 08:00, 13:30, and 21:00 add a rise. One meal each day is
  larger, so a prediabetes patient can cross 180 mg/dL after that meal
  and a diabetes patient does not stay above 180 all day.
  Steps lower glucose, short sleep raises the morning value, insulin
  widens the swing, and a small noise term is added.
  These rows follow that formula. They are not recordings from a watch.

processed/train_rows.csv
  One row every 30 minutes.
  Features end at decision_time. The label uses the following 2 hours only.
  y_glucose_30, y_glucose_60, y_glucose_90, and y_glucose_120 are targets,
  not features.
  Split is by patient: 70% train, 15% validation, 15% test, stratified
  by diabetes and prediabetes. Seed 2026.

Reproducibility
  py -3 data/generate_signals.py
  py -3 backend/src/make_dataset.py
  py -3 backend/src/train_baseline.py
  py -3 backend/src/validate_data.py
  validate_data.py resimulates the first two patients and checks every
  glucose and sleep value against signals.csv. It also recomputes their
  labels from the raw series. A mismatch exits with status 1.
  Sleep is constant inside one calendar day because it is last night's
  total, then it changes at the next midnight.
